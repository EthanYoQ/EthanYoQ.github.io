PRODUCT POSITION

以工业化流程保证长篇质量，让 AI 自主生成大纲与蓝图。

AI Novel Writer 的核心定位是确保 AI 写长篇小说的质量。普通大模型聊天缺乏全局视界，无法驾驭数十万字的长篇体量。本软件能自主生成全书大纲与精细章节蓝图，未来将进一步引入物品列表与角色关系网络。

既可直连 DeepSeek、OpenAI、Gemini 等商用云端模型，也可接入本地 Ollama 开源模型。系统围绕当前章节蓝图、相关角色资料、世界观设定与历史摘要组织上下文，配合自动化审稿报告与修稿闭环，彻底告别前后设定崩塌与角色失忆。

AI 出体力，作者出创意。自主生成的大纲、蓝图与审稿报告均由作者确认，驱动长篇小说高质量向前演进。

PRODUCT POSITION

Industrial workflow for long-form novel quality: autonomous outlines and blueprints.

AI Novel Writer's primary mission is guaranteeing novel quality over long narratives. Standard chat LLMs drift, hallucinate, and lose plot threads after a few thousand words. This workspace autonomously generates comprehensive outlines and granular chapter blueprints, with upcoming support for item inventories and character relationship networks.

Compatible with both cloud models (DeepSeek, OpenAI, Gemini) and local offline LLMs (Ollama). Context is filtered strictly around the active chapter blueprint and approved lore, followed by structured critique reports to drive iterative revision.

AI provides generative power while the author steers vision and decisions. Outlines, blueprints, and review findings keep the story strictly coherent.

TRACEABLE FLOW

从故事前提，一直走到下一章的上下文。

  1. 建立创作前提保存作品方向、关键矛盾和作者已经确认的原稿。
  2. 组织角色与世界观把人物、关系、规则和知识资料放回项目内部。
  3. 拆成大纲与章节蓝图让每一章有明确目标，而不是仅依赖一段临时提示词。
  4. 生成或导入章节草稿围绕当前章节资料调用本地或云端模型。
  5. 审稿、修稿、人工确认审稿意见可编辑和取舍，修订稿由作者决定是否合并。
  6. 形成定稿并继续定稿进入后续章节的连续性上下文。

TRACEABLE FLOW

From premise to the context that carries the next chapter.

  1. Define the premiseKeep direction, conflict, and approved manuscript material together.
  2. Build characters and world factsStore relationships, rules, and source material inside the project.
  3. Create outline and chapter blueprintsGive every chapter a deliberate target beyond a temporary prompt.
  4. Generate or import a draftCall a local or cloud model with chapter-specific context.
  5. Review, revise, approveEdit and select review notes before accepting a revision.
  6. Finalize and continueApproved copy becomes continuity context for future chapters.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

常见问题与核心特性答疑

AI Novel Writer 与普通的 AI 聊天写作有什么不同？核心定位是什么？

普通 AI 聊天写长篇容易出现前后设定矛盾、人物遗忘和剧情失控。AI Novel Writer 的核心定位是确保 AI 写长篇小说的质量。它能自主生成章节大纲与场景蓝图，未来将进一步增加物品列表与角色关系网络；让 AI 在每一章写作时都能精准遵循当前蓝图与设定，并通过自动化审稿报告指出问题驱动修稿，把长篇创作变成质量可控的工业化流水线。

它只能接入本地模型吗？支持哪些云端与本地大模型？

绝不仅限于本地模型。AI Novel Writer 既可以接入本地离线模型（通过 Ollama），也可以直连各类高性能商用云端模型（如 DeepSeek、OpenAI、Gemini、SiliconFlow）。用户可根据硬件条件与创作需求自由配置与切换，软件本身不绑定任何模型额度。

它支持直接导入已经写好的前文进行续写吗？

完全支持。支持导入历史原稿，系统会自动提取世界观事实与角色卡，并基于前文定稿建立连续性摘要，确保续写章节在人设、文风和剧情上前后统一。

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How is AI Novel Writer different from writing with a standard AI chat? What is its core focus?

Standard chat LLMs suffer from context loss, character drift, and narrative inconsistencies after a few thousand words. AI Novel Writer is dedicated to guaranteeing long-form novel quality. It autonomously generates chapter outlines and scene blueprints, with upcoming support for item inventories and character relationship graphs. Each chapter strictly follows its blueprint, accompanied by automated critique reports for iterative revision.

Is it limited to local models? Which cloud and local models are supported?

It is not restricted to local models. AI Novel Writer natively supports both high-performance cloud APIs (DeepSeek, OpenAI, Gemini, SiliconFlow) and local offline inference via Ollama. You can freely switch models depending on your compute setup and creative goals.

Can I import existing manuscripts to continue writing?

Yes. You can import previously written chapters or outlines. The system extracts world facts and characters, generating continuity summaries so subsequent chapters maintain stylistic and narrative consistency.
